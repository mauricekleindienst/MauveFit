Mauve Fit is a strength training log that keeps everything on your iPhone. No account, no ads, no tracking, and it works fully offline.

PLANS
Start with ready-made plans (Push, Pull, Legs, Full body and a home workout) or build your own: pick the exercises and set the sets, reps and rest for each one.

WORKOUT LOGGING
Log weight and reps set by set, mark warm-up sets, add sets or exercises on the fly and see what you lifted last time. The rest timer starts when you tick off a set and reminds you when it runs out, even with the app in the background.

PROGRESSION
Mauve Fit fills in your next weights: when every set reached its target, it adds one weight step; body-weight exercises go up by a rep. The suggested next plan follows recovery, so the muscles that rested longest come first.

RECORDS
Your best set of every exercise is kept with its estimated one-rep max. After each workout a summary shows duration, sets, volume and any new records.

1324 EXERCISES
A library of 1324 exercises with a muscle map of what each one trains, drawings of the start and end position for many of them, equipment and step-by-step instructions, searchable by name, muscle or equipment. Mark favourites to find them fast.

NUTRITION AND WATER
Log what you eat with calories, protein, carbs and fat against daily targets from your body weight and goal, and track your water.

PROGRESS
Charts for weekly volume and body weight, a weekly goal with a streak, and the full history of every workout and set.

In German and English, in light and dark mode.
